Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence where computers learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed.

Instead of writing rules manually, we train models using data.

🧠 Simple Definition

Machine Learning is the science of teaching computers to learn from data.

For example:

Spam email detection

Movie recommendations

Face recognition

Stock price prediction

🚀 Why ML is Popular

📊 Handles large amounts of data

🤖 Powers AI applications

💼 High industry demand

💰 Strong career opportunities

🌍 Used in almost every industry

📚 Types of Machine Learning
1️⃣ Supervised Learning

Learns from labeled data

Example: Email marked as spam/not spam

Algorithms:

Linear Regression

Logistic Regression

Decision Trees

2️⃣ Unsupervised Learning

No labeled data

Finds hidden patterns

Example:

Customer segmentation

Algorithms:

K-Means Clustering

PCA

3️⃣ Reinforcement Learning

Learns by trial and error

Used in robotics and games

Example:

Self-driving cars

Game-playing AI
